Mystica

Eleven oracles, told by candlelight.

Mystica is a candle-lit iPhone app for reflection and entertainment. You ask, the signs are drawn for real, and a narrator helps you read what fell.

How a reading unfolds

  • 1. AskChoose an oracle and put your question in your own words. A short one is enough.
  • 2. CastThe signs appear one by one: cards, lines, runes or a chart of the sky.
  • 3. InterpretA narrator weaves the signs into a reading, and you can ask it follow-up questions.

The cards, lines and stars are drawn by real chance and real calculation. The AI never picks them; it only helps you read what fell.

The Lantern

Under each sign in a reading waits a small lantern. Hold it to the light and it lets slip a glimmer: a hint, never a verdict.

A sample glimmer

Something you set down long ago is asking to be picked up, lightly, and without hurry.

The three rooms

  • OraclesPick a method. Filters and “Help me choose” find the right one.
  • CounselAsk once and let several oracles answer, then weave them into one counsel.
  • SanctumYour details, language, the narrator, your journal, and privacy.

The eleven oracles

  • TarotSeventy-eight cards on antique 1491 art.
  • I ChingCast six lines by coins or yarrow stalks.
  • AstrologyA natal chart from real astronomy.
  • NumerologyCore numbers from your name and birth date.
  • Plum BlossomMeihua Yishu: a hexagram from numbers.
  • Da Liu RenA classical Chinese timing chart.
  • Qimen DunjiaThe nine-palace board of strategy.
  • RunesElder Futhark stones, cast like dice.
  • GeomancySixteen figures built from chance.
  • LenormandThirty-six small, plain-spoken cards.
  • PalmistrySeven regions of the hand (photo reading needs a cloud key).

Private by default

Narration runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device model (iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence). There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. If you choose, you can bring your own Anthropic or OpenAI key for cloud narration; it is off until you turn it on.

For reflection and entertainment only. Readings are stories told from symbols, not predictions, and not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. Mystica is for iPhone.
